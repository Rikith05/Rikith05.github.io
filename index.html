About Me

I'm a Data Science graduate & AI Engineer from Hyderabad, India, focused on building multi-agent AI systems that are safe, observable, and production-grade.

My flagship project, SwarmForge, is an autonomous multi-agent DAG orchestrator with a 5-layer tool policy firewall that prevents hallucinated tool calls, prompt-injection escalation, credential leaks, and unbounded retry storms — achieving 100% pass rate under strict policy benchmarks.

Across internships at Ethara.ai, TensorGo, and CSIR-CSMCRI, I've worked on LLM post-training alignment, Gemma 3 fine-tuning, RAG pipelines, n8n workflow automation, and real-time computer vision.

I combine strong fundamentals in Python, Java, Spring Boot, and FastAPI with hands-on execution in LangChain, CrewAI, FAISS, Qdrant, YOLOv8, and MediaPipe.
